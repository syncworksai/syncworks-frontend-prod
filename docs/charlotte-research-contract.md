# Charlotte research-only pilot
Charlotte must never place, modify, cancel, or authorize orders, access brokerage trading credentials, or invoke execution tools. A separate future execution service is outside this scope. All alerts are requests for human review, never execution instructions.
Pilot access must be enforced by authenticated server-side allowlist; no private portfolio or account details in public source.
## Evidence contract
- Chart inputs: timestamped, split-adjusted OHLCV with interval, source, session and delay metadata. Compute SMA 20/50/200, EMA, RSI 14 (Wilder), MACD, ATR, relative volume; insufficient observations yield unavailable, not invented values.
- Fundamentals: SEC filings and licensed earnings data; GAAP diluted EPS versus adjusted EPS explicitly distinguished; quarterly and trailing 12 months, reported period and publication date; estimate comparisons must match basis. Revenue, margins, free cash flow, debt, P/E and sector peers. Negative earnings yield unavailable P/E.
- Macro: official release timestamps and revisions, consumer credit, delinquency, yields, inflation, employment. Separate causal hypothesis from observed association.
- News and social: cite source links, distinguish primary evidence, commentary and unverified claims. Respect provider access rules.
## Output
Plain-language summary first; expandable technical and fundamental evidence. Weekly/daily trend and four-hour setup where data is available. Bull/bear/base scenarios, conditional entry review zone, potential resistance targets, invalidation, upcoming events, uncertainty, and reasons to wait. Never describe hypothetical targets as guaranteed. Stale or missing quote data blocks current timing assessments.
Calendar/Chief of Staff handoff is limited to user-authorized review reminders; deduplicate alerts and show source timestamps.
Share research indicators with Day Trade but never link Charlotte to execution. ElevenLabs narrates the same sourced report. No device voice substitution without disclosure.
